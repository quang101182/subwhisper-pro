Frequently asked questions

What audio and video formats are supported?
SubWhisper Pro supports all major formats: MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, WEBM for video, and MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A for audio. Files up to 5 GB are supported. The audio is extracted client-side before being sent to the AI engine.
How many languages are available for translation?
SubWhisper Pro supports 75+ languages for translation, including specialized handling for CJK languages (Chinese, Japanese, Korean) with proper honorifics, writing systems, and cultural context. Transcription is available in 100+ languages via Whisper and Universal-3 engines.
How does the 14-day free trial work?
When you sign up, you get 14 days of access to all Pro features, including 2 hours of transcription, 10 transcriptions and 100 translations. A card is required at checkout, but it is charged only after the trial ends. You can cancel anytime during the trial period and you won't be charged at all.
Are my files secure and private?
Yes. Your video and audio files are processed entirely in your browser. Only the extracted audio is sent to the AI transcription API, and it is not stored after processing. No video is ever uploaded to our servers. SubWhisper Pro is a privacy-first tool.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your Polar customer portal. There are no cancellation fees and no long-term commitments. If you cancel, you retain access until the end of your current billing period.
What's the difference with the free version of SubWhisper?
The free version supports 12 translation languages and basic transcription. SubWhisper Pro adds 75+ languages, multi-pass AI translation for higher accuracy, AI cleanup to fix hallucinations, ASS export format, foreign language detection, and priority processing. It's built for professionals who need reliable, production-ready subtitles.
